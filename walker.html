Settings

Graphics

High and Ultra add sun shadows, grass, rocks and bushes, and Cinematic effects (glow, film curve, lens fringe, grain). Lower the quality or the view distance if the game runs slowly.

Controls and sound

Controls

W A S DWalk. In a car: accelerate, steer, brake and reverse.
MouseLook. Click the view to capture the mouse.
Shift / SpaceRun / jump. In a car, Space is the handbrake.
EGet in or out of your car, a bicycle, a helicopter or a plane when you stand next to one.
HelicopterSpace up, C or Shift down, W S fly, A D turn.
PlaneW S throttle, Space nose up, C or Shift nose down, A D bank. Needs about 100 km/h to lift off.
WhereHelipads and runways dot the countryside, and a bicycle waits near where you start. Only the car can be called to you.
CavesDark mouths in hillsides lead underground: caverns, old mines and buried vaults. Your lantern lights the way, and relics wait in the deep. Walk back to the glowing doorway to leave.
GoalsThe card at the left shows your current goal and the compass shows where to go. Finish them in order to reach the ship and launch to Chapter 2. E at the rocket boards it.
Click / 1-4 / Q / wheelSwing or fire, pick a slot (fists, blade, taser, medkit), cycle items. Wolves and bandits roam after you find a sword; caves hold spiders and bats.
EAlso gathers glowing forage specks, drinks at water, and cooks at a campfire.
N / XHandheld map (hold it while you walk) / drop a waypoint where you are looking. Shift+X removes the nearest. Comma and period zoom the hand map. On the big map (M) click to add or remove waypoints.
CCrafting and advancements, in the journal.
B / R / ZBuild a campfire (3 wood, 2 stone) / eat / rest at a fire until morning. Watch food, water and warmth.
[ ]Narrow / widen the field of view.
FFlashlight. It switches on by itself after dark and in caves.
VSwitch between first and third person, to see your walker.
MWorld map, with states, roads and cities.
J / PJournal / photo mode. The world keeps running.
EscPause menu.

On a touch screen, the left stick moves, drag anywhere else to look, and the round buttons jump or drive.

Map

North is up. Squares are cities, circles are towns and villages, triangles are ruins. Teal means you have been there. Black dots are caves you have entered. Purple marks helipads (circles) and runways (bars). Yellow lines are roads and orange lines are highways. The red arrow is you, pointing the way you face. Orange diamonds are your waypoints (click the map to add or remove one). Teal triangle is your goal, orange dots are camp fires.
